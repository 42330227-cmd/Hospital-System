# Hospital-System
Developed a hospital platform with patient and doctor authentication, specialty based doctor search, appointment reservations, doctor availability management, and a smart queue system that allows patients to track their position and appointments.

# Hospital Management and Smart Queue System

A full-stack **Hospital Management and Smart Queue System** designed to simplify the process of booking medical appointments and managing patient queues. The system provides separate accounts and dashboards for **patients and doctors**, allowing each user to access features based on their role.

Main Features

Patient Registration and Login: Patients can create an account, log in securely, and manage their personal information

Doctor Registration and Login: Doctors have their own accounts and dashboards where they can manage their availability, specialties, and appointments

Doctor Search: Patients can search for doctors based on their medical specialty, such as orthopedics, cardiology, dermatology, neurology, dentistry, and more

Doctor Availability: Patients can see which doctors are currently available and choose an appropriate appointment time

Online Reservation: Patients can reserve an appointment with an available doctor directly through the system

Smart Queue System: After booking an appointment, patients receive a queue number and can monitor their position in the queue without needing to wait continuously inside the hospital

Appointment Management: Patients can view upcoming and previous appointments, while doctors can view and manage their scheduled patients

Doctor Dashboard: Doctors can see their queue, upcoming appointments, patient information, and update their availability

Patient Dashboard: Patients can manage reservations, view queue status, and receive updates about their appointments

Notifications: The system can notify patients about appointment confirmations, queue updates, cancellations, and changes in doctor availability

Admin Dashboard: Hospital administrators can manage doctors, patients, specialties, appointments, queues, and hospital departments

Example Workflow

A patient logs into the system and selects **Orthopedics** because they have a bone-related problem. The system displays available orthopedic doctors along with their available appointment times. The patient selects a doctor and reserves an appointment.

After the reservation, the system automatically assigns the patient a **queue number**. The patient can then follow the queue through their dashboard and receive notifications when their turn is approaching.

The goal of the project is to create a modern digital hospital platform that reduces waiting times, organizes doctor appointments, and provides patients and hospital staff with an efficient way to manage medical visits.
